Stock Compensation & Capital Gains

Employer stock awards and investment gains have unique tax rules. Track them carefully.

How Stock Compensation Is Taxed

RSUs / Stock Awards (Public Co.): Taxed as employment income at vest. Full FMV added to T4 box 14 + box 38. Subsequent gains = capital gains.

Stock Options (Public): Bargain element = employment income at exercise. May qualify for 50% deduction (110(1)(d)) if exercise price ≥ FMV at grant.

ESPP: Discount on purchase = taxable employment benefit. Holding period after purchase determines capital gains treatment.

⚠ Sale of stock at a gain triggers capital gains; loss may trigger superficial loss rules if you repurchase within 30 days.
